# EV Market Analysis: Electric Vehicle Market Insights

An interactive Business Intelligence and data analytics project on the global Electric Vehicle (EV) industry built using Tableau Public.

🔗 Live Interactive Demo
Tableau Public Story & Dashboard: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/rudraneel.simaiya/viz/EVMarketAnalysis-ElectricVehicleMarketInsights/EVMarketStory

📌 Project Overview
The electric vehicle market has seen rapid growth across diverse vehicle categories, battery chemistries, and regional markets. Automakers, business analysts, and policymakers require data-driven intelligence to evaluate market share, monitor battery performance, and identify consumer preferences.

This project delivers an interactive multi-view Tableau dashboard and story addressing three primary business scenarios:
1. Emily Johnson (EV Market Strategy Director): High-growth vehicle category volume and annual sales distribution.
2. David Chen (Automotive BI Manager):** Leading manufacturer performance, market share, and regional production hubs.
3. Dr. Sarah Williams (Sustainable Mobility Research Analyst): Battery chemistry driving range benchmarks and customer ratings.

📊 Key Insights Supported by Data
Top Vehicle Category by Volume: Two-Wheeler Scooters lead annual sales (~85.03M units), followed by Sedans (~78.31M units) and Compact SUVs (~76.12M units).
Leading Manufacturers: SurgeWorks leads sales volume (~4.88M units), followed closely by CurrentMobility and ElektraMobility.
Battery Chemistry Range: Average driving range across standard chemistries (LFP, NCA, NMC, Sodium-Ion) clusters around ~324 km.
Global Manufacturing: Global production volume is distributed across major hubs including China, Japan, Vietnam, the UK, and the USA.
